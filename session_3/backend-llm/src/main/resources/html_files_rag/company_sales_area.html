The Quarterly Victory Celebration

The Sales Division is famous throughout Example Company for its most beloved tradition. Whenever the company successfully meets its quarterly revenue goals, the team organizes an official company celebration on the first Wednesday of the next financial quarter.

Employees eagerly look forward to this tradition, as each celebration takes place at a large event venue featuring an enormous swimming pool, outdoor recreation areas, music, food, and activities for employees and their families.

Goal Achieved = Time To Celebrate!

Guests of Honor

One of the most unique aspects of the celebration is the presence of real ducks and ducklings that participate in carefully planned educational and recreational activities during the event.

Employees can relax by the pool, observe the ducklings, learn about waterfowl care, and enjoy a day focused on community, teamwork, and appreciation for another successful quarter.

Animal welfare is always the highest priority. All ducks and ducklings are accompanied by professional veterinary contractors who oversee their care throughout the event.

🦆👨‍⚕️👩‍⚕️

The Legendary Duck Vets

Over the years, another beloved tradition has emerged. The veterinary specialists who supervise the animals often arrive wearing cheerful duck-themed costumes and accessories while performing their professional duties.

Their appearance has become one of the highlights of the event, delighting employees and children alike while helping reinforce the company's playful culture.

Many employees claim the celebration is not officially underway until the first duck veterinarian appears in costume and announces, "The ducks have arrived!"

Department Culture

The Sales Division combines ambition with celebration. Team members work hard to achieve challenging objectives, but they also believe milestones should be recognized and shared with the broader company.

Their philosophy is simple: When the company succeeds, everyone should have a chance to celebrate together.
