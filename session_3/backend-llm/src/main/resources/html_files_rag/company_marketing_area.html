Sky-Scale Creativity

The Marketing Division became famous after launching several highly unusual publicity events. One of the team's most celebrated projects involved coordinating aircraft to create a giant duck shape across the sky using environmentally friendly skywriting techniques.

The event attracted thousands of spectators and inspired countless photographs, instantly becoming part of company lore.

🚁✨🦆

Ducks Made Of Light

Another iconic campaign featured hundreds of synchronized drones carrying LED lights. During evening performances, the drones assembled into the Example Company duck logo, the company name, and a variety of entertaining animated shapes.

Children especially enjoy the shows, which often include giant glowing ducklings, floating hearts, stars, rainbows, and scenes inspired by the company's most popular rubber duck products.

Athletes and Ambassadors

Numerous members of the Marketing Division also participate in competitive sports at amateur and professional levels. Over the years, several employees have developed large followings across social media platforms.

Whether competing in marathons, cycling events, triathlons, sailing races, or winter sports, they proudly wear equipment featuring the Example Company duck logo.

Fans often recognize the brand immediately and associate it with positivity, creativity, and joyful surprises.
