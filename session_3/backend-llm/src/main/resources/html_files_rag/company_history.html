Our History

The story of Example Company is built on a simple belief: even the smallest products can create unforgettable moments of happiness.

2012 • Humble Beginnings

Example Company was founded by a small team of designers and toy enthusiasts who wanted to transform ordinary rubber ducks into products filled with personality, creativity, and charm.

2015 • Growing Popularity

Our unique duck designs quickly gained attention in local gift shops and specialty stores. Customers loved the combination of quality craftsmanship and playful themes.

2018 • Expanding the Collection

We introduced themed collections, seasonal editions, and limited-release ducks for collectors, expanding our reach to new markets and communities around the world.

2022 • International Recognition

Example Company became a recognized name among rubber duck enthusiasts, with products reaching customers across multiple countries and inspiring a vibrant collector culture.

Today • Looking Ahead

While we have grown significantly since our first duck, our purpose remains unchanged: bringing smiles, laughter, and imagination to people of all ages through the world's most delightful rubber ducks.
